# Product Vision

**Product:** AbhiKnow. **Tagline:** The World's Knowledge. One Intelligent Companion.
**Mission:** Make reliable knowledge discovery, research, reading and personalized learning accessible to everyone.

## What it is

A research and learning platform where one search finds books, papers, courses, videos, audio, news and code, then shows how each can be legally accessed. The long-term goal is a unified path: **Discover → Explore → Access → Read → Understand → Practice → Research → Master**.

## Immediate goal

Be genuinely valuable to the first hundred users. Scale ambitions follow evidence, not the other way round.

## Principles

1. **Free-first.** No mandatory paid APIs, databases or services. Every dependency's limits and licence are recorded in [LICENSING](LICENSING.md).
2. **Open-source-first.** Prefer maintained, clearly licensed, portable tools.
3. **Discovery-first.** Search quality, metadata accuracy and access classification come before any chatbot.
4. **AI-independent.** Search, filters, library, reading, notes and citations work with no LLM.
5. **Independent product.** Own repo, database, backend, frontend, auth and deployment.
6. **Incremental.** One verified vertical slice at a time. No fake buttons, no invented results.
7. **Globally accessible.** Low bandwidth, ordinary phones, assistive technology, many languages (added only when implemented).
8. **Responsible scalability.** Modular monolith plus workers now; split components later when justified.

## Legal and trust stance

AbhiKnow never bypasses paywalls, DRM, CAPTCHAs, waiting periods or authentication. Free to see does not mean free to copy. Sites known for unauthorized distribution are never approved providers; the product instead surfaces lawful alternatives (open-access versions, library borrowing, publisher links).

## Differentiators

Universal multi-format discovery; transparent access classification; authorized access orchestration; unified reading workspace; source-grounded AI; personalized learning paths; Research Completeness Dashboard; knowledge-gap detection; continuous monitoring; affordability.
